How the pilot works

  1. Intake
    You provide the checkpoint, ComfyUI details, catalog objective, and acceptance reviewer.
  2. Baseline
    We render the fixed prompt pack with your unmodified workflow and record the protocol.
  3. Kernel run
    We run the CalibrixKernelScale node with the same prompts and seed sequence.
  4. Review
    Holdout metrics and a blind human comparison determine ACCEPTED, REVIEW, or BLOCKED.

Acceptance gates

  • Every requested image renders without runtime errors.
  • ComfyUI confirms the Calibrix node is installed and used.
  • Primary holdout score improves by at least 0.01 under the agreed scorer.
  • No secondary holdout metric regresses beyond 0.01.
  • Kernel wins or ties at least 80% of blinded buyer/reviewer comparisons.
  • Zero critical defects under the buyer's declared product checklist.
