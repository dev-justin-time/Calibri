Architectural Bounds, Explicit Non-Goals & Implementation Disclosures

F028: Custom Scorer Hooks

Calibrix will never execute un-sandboxed Python bytecode in production nodes. User-provided custom scorers must compile to a restricted pure-math AST schema or emit gRPC requests over localhost isolated enclaves.

F089: Envelope Encryption

Client master keys (CMKs) are never cached in plaintext. Key-wrapping uses AES-256-GCM via external HSM (AWS KMS / Vault). In-memory parameters are zeroized immediately after graph compilation.

F100: ZK-Style Attestation

F100 provides cryptographic Pedersen commitment hashes and challenge-response verification. It does not construct multi-gigabyte Groth16 zk-SNARK circuits for billion-parameter model graphs.

F080: EU Annex IV Boundary

Annex IV outputs provide technical logging, energy telemetry, and mathematical conformity proofs. They serve as audit documentation but do not replace certified legal counsel or statutory notified body audits.
